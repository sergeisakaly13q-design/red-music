# Yandex Music import

Red Music supports importing public Yandex Music playlists by link.

Flow:
1. Library -> Import playlist -> Yandex Music.
2. Paste a public playlist URL.
3. Click "Найти треки".
4. Save the imported playlist.

Imported tracks that are not present in the Red Music catalog are retained as external tracks with their Yandex Music link.

## VIP offline rule

- VIP and higher roles can download tracks that are already hosted in the Red Music catalog and listen to those downloaded files offline.
- Free/User accounts can play available Red Music tracks online but cannot use offline downloads.
- External tracks from Yandex Music, YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, or other third-party services are not downloaded by Red Music. They remain external links.

Yandex Music playlist access uses a public/internal endpoint and may require maintenance if Yandex changes that endpoint.
